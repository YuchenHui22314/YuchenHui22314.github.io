On the first day of the new year, I dreamed I traveled 10 years into the future. While traveling, my soul drifted out onto the street. Everyone seemed to be moving in slow motion, and only I wandered around like a lonely ghost (shouldn’t it be the other way around?). After the trip I met XYF. She had traveled here too. She complained that she had traveled many times, but she wasn’t happy with how any of them turned out. After traveling, I was still thinking about how to study for the gaokao (the Chinese university entrance exam), especially physics and chemistry. I wasn’t happy either, so I traveled a few more times. Along the way, I actually ran into XYF from one of her earlier trips. I told her the story of how I had met the later her. She seemed to understand something.
