In the later part of the dream, when we went outside, the night sky was dazzling. Something bright was moving around with no pattern at all. Is this what Van Gogh saw in *The Starry Night*? I looked more closely, and my last hope died. It was the other planets of the solar system, flying around the night sky like balloons letting out air! Each planet looked about 10 times the size of the Moon. They must have been very close to Earth already. Suddenly Jupiter stopped moving. It quickly grew bigger in my view, and its brown bands were bright and clear. That meant Jupiter was rushing straight at us! The strange part was that Jupiter then started to shrink as it came closer. In the end it turned into a shooting star and flew below the horizon. This felt a bit eerie. Even if the impact point wasn’t on our side of the Earth, we should still have seen Jupiter, because it is so big. Soon a dust cloud like a nuclear blast shot up at the horizon, followed by a deep rumbling. OK, looks like Earth is done for… But something still felt wrong. Wasn’t the script supposed to be “Earth falls into Jupiter”? How did it turn into “Jupiter hits Earth”?
