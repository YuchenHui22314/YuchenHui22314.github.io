Today, in the first dream, a group of us went on a trip to Toronto and stayed in an Airbnb. In the early evening, a faint blue was still left in the sky, and snowflakes started to fall. I noticed right away: this was snow falling from a clear sky! I slapped my thigh. This is lake-effect snow! A cold wave is passing through these days, and Lake Ontario is right next to us, so it must be lake-effect snow! At that thought, I rushed out of the Airbnb in excitement to embrace the snow filling the sky. The sky was now split in two. One half was in the blue hour of the evening. The other half held low gray clouds moving closer. I thought these low clouds might not be clouds at all, but dense ice crystals in the air. I knew heavier snow was coming. I went back inside first and waited a while. Then MK suddenly called me outside, all excited, offering to teach me to “fly”. A bit puzzled, I followed and found that the snowstorm outside had gone into rage mode: lots of whirlwinds of ice crystals were tearing around in different places! MK jumped into one of the whirlwinds, floated up at once, and could then steer freely and move sideways. I quickly jumped into another whirlwind and started flying freely! I flew into a small wood nearby and saw a few whirlwinds tearing through it. It was a bit scary…
