Today I dreamed I was eating at an open-air canteen with A, H and others. In the dream, the school gave each of us a high-tech gadget. It looked like a futuristic hardcover book with a metal finish. The gadget could turn into all kinds of tableware. A seemed to make a dry pot and a canteen tray. Both were in the same metallic gold-and-silver colors. Even more amazing, the book could also make food! I copied the others and made my book change shape too, but mine never looked as fancy as theirs. Soon the people around me started exploring more wonderful uses of the book, like making a model, or anything they wanted. It was basically a wish-granting machine.
