Today, in the first dream, I was swimming at an indoor pool. It seemed to be about to close, and only one corner of the pool had some swimmers gathered in it. I jumped in too, but after swimming further out I found the water getting colder and colder, especially deeper down. Then I saw a water inlet where the swimmers were gathered. I thought, the warm water must be coming out here, and the rest of the pool is probably all cold. So I started swimming back. Since I was doing breaststroke, my legs were quite far below the surface and felt the biting cold. I also found that the colder the water, the harder I had to kick. By the time I got back to the swimmers, my thighs were really sore. I thought, is this why people get cramps so easily when swimming in cold water?

In the second dream, I was eating at an open-plan restaurant. I ordered a fish and a piece of cheese. The cheese was soft and fluffy to bite, a lot like bread, and it didn’t have the heavy, cloying taste of normal cheese. It was very light. In the dream I was very happy with it.

After that, I seemed to be playing a chess-like board game again, this time on a barbecue grill. My opponent (G?) was very confident at first, because he had lots of chopsticks to attack me with. But he soon stopped laughing, because I used a trade-pieces tactic. On my side, I had hidden lots of plastic figurines (like the Seer ones from my childhood) under some plates on the surface. Each figurine could take out one chopstick, going down together with it. My figurines took up less space than his chopsticks, so right from the start his forces dropped sharply, and he soon gave up.
