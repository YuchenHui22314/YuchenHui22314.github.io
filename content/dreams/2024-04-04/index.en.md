The scene switched to a third-person view. I was something like Guo Jing, my mom was Guo Jing’s mother, and there was also Yang Kang. But unlike in The Legend of the Condor Heroes, where Yang Kang and Guo Jing are both human, in this dream my mom and I were a kind of primitive creature that couldn’t talk, while the Yang family were a very advanced kind of creature that could fly. A volcano was about to erupt, and once it did, we would all die. Then Yang Kang said they couldn’t abandon Guo Jing’s family (no wait, it should be Yang Tiexin), and came to warn us so we could be saved. One fun detail: since in this world we couldn’t talk, once Yang Tiexin found us, he could only use gestures to tell us to run. He made all kinds of exaggerated poses, bending his body into a bow and then running the other way, looping around in a U shape.

In the end we obviously understood. Somehow we could suddenly fly too, so I flew after him to the other side of the canyon. Actually, it wasn’t really a canyon: we lived in a side branch of a big river valley, a small valley of its own. Now we flew into the big valley. On its bank, a volcano had already erupted. A column of smoke was rising, the sky was full of smoke, you could see the haze with your own eyes, and visibility was dropping. We headed for the other side of the valley.
