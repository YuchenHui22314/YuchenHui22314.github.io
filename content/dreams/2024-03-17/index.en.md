I dreamed I was on the Chinese national badminton team. After training I walked into the locker room and found Li Shifeng getting ready to shower in the bathing area in the middle of the locker room. I wasn’t going to shower, so I went straight to the bench next to the showers and sat down to get dressed; I’d shower at home. I put on my long johns first, then my trousers. But before the trousers I forgot to put on socks to hold the long johns in place, so I took the half-on trousers off again to put on my socks. After that I kept sorting my things: what to take home went into my backpack, and what to keep went into the locker. Somehow I had a lot of stuff, and it took me forever. Meanwhile Li Shifeng had finished showering, sat down on the bench next to me to change, got ready to go, and in the end left before me. I felt really awkward; I was just too slow. When I finally got out, I saw Li Shifeng, Huang Dongping and Feng Yanzhe sitting around a table eating. I went over to look. Ha! They had ordered a few stir-fried dishes and a bowl of rice each, and were about to dig in. One dish was pork stir-fried with green peppers, and it looked really good! I happily sat down to join them, but then noticed they only had three bowls of rice. I thought, how inconsiderate, they didn’t even think of me! So I went out to the canteen to order for myself. I wanted to find the stir-fry window to order a few dishes, but I didn’t see one at a glance. Then I noticed a strange structure in the corner. I went over and found AD there too. It turned out to be a counter where you order food by playing a game, and this round structure was the game area. Without thinking twice, and without learning the rules, we just started playing. Inside was a circular conveyor belt divided into small squares, with shiny “points” floating above each square. In the area inside the belt there were some dishes. I thought, I just need to step on the dishes to order them, right? So I strode into the middle and started stomping, like whack-a-mole with my feet. But the dishes I stepped on slowly disappeared, which left me puzzled. So I went onto the conveyor belt to collect points instead. Like in a rhythm game, I had to match my footwork to the squares to score. It was really like the ladder footwork drills in badminton, so I focused and passed easily, and said proudly to AD next to me: “We badminton players have the edge now!” But even after all that stepping, I didn’t get any dishes. I was very confused, so I watched how others played. A little girl first stepped on a few squares on the belt, then walked onto a springboard at its edge. The springboard bounced and threw her onto a dish, and only then did it count as ordered. Now I understood, but I didn’t play anymore. Then I saw an L-shaped counter at the canteen entrance selling char siu, and I got interested right away. I took a plate to the short side of the counter and ordered some char siu, then saw braised chicken with bamboo shoots and ordered that too. The bamboo shoots were cut as wide as the teeth of a comb, and the chicken was fine thigh meat; I couldn’t help imagining crunchy shoots with tender, smooth chicken. Around the corner of the L, I also ordered some beef tripe, the white kind, a lot like the tripe in pho. Since I already had two meats, I asked the canteen lady for a smaller portion. To balance meat and vegetables, I also asked for stir-fried broccoli. Strangely, when serving the broccoli she seemed reluctant to give me much. I was very puzzled: it’s a vegetable dish, no need for the famous ladle-shaking trick… Last came the rice. She was going to take a new bowl, but for some reason there was already a used bowl on my tray (with leftover rice and sauce still on it), so I asked her to serve it in that. My bowl was actually shaped like a cup, tall and thin. Somehow, once she filled it, the bowl turned transparent, and I saw rice porridge inside. I thought, what, there was no water in the pot… But whatever. Just as I was carrying my tray off to eat (still wondering why I didn’t have to pay), two canteen managers suddenly came in and started yelling at the staff. One manager was French, the other Chinese. They told the staff that after two o’clock they couldn’t sell anymore; they were closing. A staff member argued back: what about all the food left over? A manager threw something straight into the pot in front and said, throw it all out! I thought, are they crazy? and hurried off to eat. I was in a hurry because in the dream I could already feel I was about to wake up, and I was trying as hard as I could to stay asleep until I got to eat the good food. But just then I turned around and ran into a mother and her son. The boy was pestering his mom to buy him a roast chicken. I tried with all my might not to listen to them, but I failed. In the end I woke up without eating a single bite.
