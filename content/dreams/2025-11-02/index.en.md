Today I dreamed I was in a chemistry lab, where Dad and Mom were playing Chinese chess. Some pieces were normal pieces, and some were flasks, test tubes and so on. There was also a magical rule: these lab tools could sit on small plates as pieces, and to make a move you moved the plate, and the piece on it moved along. Dad and Mom each attacked on one side. Soon Mom’s plate reached the gate of Dad’s general. The rules said that if the liquid in a beaker on a plate splashed out and touched the general, that also counted as échec et mat. In other words, Mom needed only one move to checkmate. But as far as I could see, Dad still needed several moves. It looked like the game was decided.
