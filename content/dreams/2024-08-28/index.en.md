Today I dreamed Mom and I drove out to stay at a hotel. Right after we arrived, I saw a huge thunderstorm cloud outside, and under it a funnel seemed about to touch the ground. Just as I was about to shout for everyone to run, the world suddenly seemed to speed up, like a video on fast-forward: the storm cloud in front of me started changing faster and faster. In the end the funnel cloud didn’t touch down and faded away in the air, but the whole cloud layer seemed to be pressing down, reaching toward the ground with many tentacle-like clouds with hexagonal cross-sections (picture the shape a snowflake traces as it slides through space, a 3D pattern). These downward tentacle clouds soon filled the space between the cloud base and the ground. Then I heard someone nearby shout, “Downburst!” I thought, so this is what a downburst looks like… Later the storm cloud quickly cleared away, and it felt like a whole age had passed. Soon Mom and I were driving again through the city at night.
