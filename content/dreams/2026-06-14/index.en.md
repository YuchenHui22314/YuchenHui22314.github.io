Today I dreamed I was taking a training course. But I suddenly realized it was a pyramid scheme, and that we would be killed in the afternoon class. So during the lunch break, a friend and I slipped out of the building, ready to run. Our escape route went along a stretch of coast. Suddenly the tide came in. Huge waves came at us from every side and pounded the rocks around us, like on a typhoon day. The water kept closing in, so we had to start climbing the steep cliffs on the shore. I climbed up a very tall stone pillar, but in the end I lost my footing and fell… At the very moment I died from the fall, I was reborn. I was back in the pyramid scheme’s classroom, and I started planning my escape again. This time I brought 4 friends, called a, b and c. In the morning the pyramid scheme had handed out milk, so we planned to take the milk with us. That way we wouldn’t run out of food. We got moving right away. This time we didn’t waste any time and crossed the coast quickly, before the tide came in. We reached the side of a church. The Communauto car I had booked was parked right there. Just as we were about to get in, the pyramid scheme people came to catch us! It turned out that b and c, two of the four of us, were moles for the pyramid scheme. Then a suddenly gave me a look, and it all became clear to me. If we pretended that we had planned the escape to test how loyal b and c were, we could clear our names! So we turned their trick back on them and fooled the half-convinced pyramid scheme crew. But this time I also noticed that b and c had a green ring of light around them, while a, who was on my side, had a rainbow-colored ring (what is this, Instagram?). That was probably how to tell friend from foe. When we got back to the building where the classes were, a and I started our escape again. In an atrium like a big shopping mall, we climbed down along the railings floor by floor, like a classic chase scene in a movie. After this quick descent, we had shaken off almost everyone chasing us…
