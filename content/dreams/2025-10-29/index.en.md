Today I dreamed I had the Eye of the Future and the Eye of History. The Eye of the Future let me pick the future I liked from the distribution of the next hour. The Eye of History could show what anything looked like at any time in the past, however long ago. Having these two divine tools meant I was now truly all-knowing and all-powerful. At school I showed my classmates my miracles: wherever the beanbags they threw flew, I always caught them cleanly in the end.
