Today I dreamed I went into a building to get my badminton rackets restrung (the same badminton building I’ve dreamed about several times before). There were 6 or 7 of them, which seemed to be everything I owned. After the restringing, I went to a street training camp. Someone had put up a net on a city square along a main road, and people were playing pickup games. I rode there on my bike, the one with a basket. When I got there, I parked the bike, left my racket bag in the basket, and took one racket over to play. But while playing, I found that my racket had turned into a palm-leaf fan! I had no idea what was going on, so I took another one. This time it turned into something like a piece of cloth. Every time, it turned into something light and soft. I was coming completely unstrung. When I went back to my bike, the racket bag was gone. It was hanging in a tree, and every racket inside had broken strings! My freshly strung rackets! I wondered whether the strings broke because… it was too cold? But then how did the bag end up in a tree… After the event, my mom and I headed home together. Halfway back, I suddenly remembered I hadn’t taken my racket bag, so we started crossing the road. (I dreamed a very clear scene of crossing the road.)
