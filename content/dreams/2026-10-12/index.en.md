In the second dream, I was doing some task for Professor X that needed a literature review. When I got to the lab in the morning, Professor X was already there. When he saw me, he asked what time it was. I said it was already 11. Professor X was very surprised. He thought it was evening. I thought, Professor X, don’t tell me you pulled another all-nighter?? He smiled and said he had just finished reading papers. I thought, it must be what M said: he updates his own knowledge base regularly… He asked what I was doing. I said it was the task he gave me, and I needed to see how other people write surveys. As I said this, I took out a printed paper and went blah blah about the red-and-green figures on it. Halfway through, I suddenly realized I hadn’t seen how they evaluated the quality of the survey generated at the end. I was quite scared Professor X would ask. But Professor X thought about it very differently. He went to the corner of the desk, took out a sheet of scratch paper, drew 5 circles on it, #1 to #5, and started studying the Markov chain proposed in the paper. I leaned in to get a closer look. Right then, a person in a black hat sitting next to me suddenly turned around. It was YX, and I hadn’t even recognized them. Then another professor, a foreigner, suddenly appeared at the lab door. Like a magic trick, he turned the door he was holding on to into a train door. It was a “hybrid” train door. The left half was a wooden door like on a high-speed train. The right half was a gray door like on the old sleeper trains, with a handle. The professor asked us a question: which way should this door be pulled? I thought to myself, it should be the way that is easier to pull toward your body… to the right? YX was thinking hard too. The professor suddenly gave a wicked smile. The green light of an automatic sensor came on, and the door slid open by itself into the walls on both sides! He burst out laughing and said, your thinking is still too rigid, to think about this problem you should blah blah xxxxx, and started telling us a whole bunch of academic ideas. In the dream I felt my brain start racing, and I woke up right away…
