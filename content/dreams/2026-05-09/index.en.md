Today I dreamed I was selected for the university team (together with B). Right at the start, one of the senior players showed us a way to serve with playing cards (?). You hold a stack of cards in front of you with both hands and split it into two sub-stacks with your left and right hands. There seemed to be some kind of tension between the two sub-stacks. Then the card between them would shoot out with great force, somehow turn into a shuttlecock, and start falling near the net. I thought, such an advanced training method? This is pure black-magic tech. We newcomers started trying, but I couldn’t get the hang of it. A senior player came over to coach us and said to use the middle finger of the left hand to flick a card from the two stacks into the middle, then launch it with soft power, the kind where four ounces move a thousand pounds. I tried for a long time, but every card I sent out was limp. While I still couldn’t figure it out, another senior player, a woman, had already started working on a wooden frame. The whole frame lay flat in one plane, and the next step seemed to be stretching white cloth over it. This machine also seemed to be for serving. After all her work, we just stared, jaws on the floor. When we tried to go and ask her about it, she ignored us… I suddenly had a feeling we were about to be left out. Sure enough, a little later the four of us at mid-to-high C level were put in a separate group to train on our own, while the A–B level players trained by themselves. I thought, fine, we’ll train on our own then. So the four of us started training. After a while the coach suddenly vanished. One of us, a guy from abroad, went to ask on the next court and found that everyone there had left. I went over to ask what was going on, and he instantly switched to Chinese: “I guess their training is over. I think we can leave too.” Wait, how is this blond, blue-eyed guy’s Chinese so good?? Speechless, I kept chatting with him as we walked out. On the way past the security office at the door, we asked the guard why we couldn’t use the team’s own GPU computing resources…
