Today I dreamed I played Kunlavut in a best-of-three match, with 31 points per game. After two games it was 1–1. For the third game our court was gone, so the match moved to my home. The study window was the net. I stood inside, and Kunlavut stood outside in the stainless-steel security cage over the window. In the dream the cage was very big, but it had just rained and snowed, and with all sorts of junk on it, it was covered in ice. I served a sneaky flick to the back court. It went out, but Kun still went for it. He slipped and flew backward. I have no idea how his body got through such narrow gaps between the bars, but it looked like he was about to fall off the building! I panicked and rushed over to grab him. Luckily Kun grabbed the bars with his hands, so he didn’t get reborn as Zhu Bajie the pig. After I pulled him back up, we got ready to find another court and keep playing. Kun said he would go to the court first and wait for me. But when I turned around, I saw that Kun had already posted our result in the group chat: the third game stopped at 18:16. I figured he was probably too shaken and was going to retire from the match…
