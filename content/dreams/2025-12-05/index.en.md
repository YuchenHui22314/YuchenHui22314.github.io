Today I dreamed my high school was holding a New Year’s Eve gala on the summit of Mount Everest. I was in a performance group and had to put on a show. We first rehearsed at an Everest base camp, then hurried up when it was almost time. The mountain path was wrapped in clouds and mist. On the summit there was a small platform where the school had put up a big black tent, and the gala was held inside it. I stood on a rock by the path and looked out over the land below. In the blue hour, the moon had just risen over the horizon in front of me. Like a faint candle, it could only outline a little of the misty Himalayas. But the unusual sense of open space convinced me that I really was standing on top of the world. Suddenly a strange feeling spread through my mind: why was there no snow here? Places this high and cold should have snow all year. But thanks to my “rich knowledge of geography”, I instantly “grasped” the “truth”: because of Earth’s axial tilt, the circle Everest travels as Earth spins sits at an angle to Earth’s orbit around the Sun. So during each turn, Everest is sometimes in the Northern Hemisphere, sometimes on the equator, and sometimes in the Southern Hemisphere. Right now Everest must be moving northeast. Soon it would reach about where Japan is, then keep going north to the Sea of Okhotsk, Kamchatka and so on, and finally pass over the Arctic Circle before heading south again. Haha, by then, inside the Arctic Circle, there would definitely be snow, but for now it was still in the temperate zone, so there was no snow. Once I had figured this out, I felt a little smug. Haha, such a small problem, solved with just a bit of thinking.

I happily walked into the tent and saw some people I knew, like LMZ and ZY, sitting in the simple audience seats (a bit like the seating at CIKM). I went over and sat with them. The gala had been going on for a while. In one act, two classmates who had both been victims of PUA (emotional manipulation) went on stage to tell their stories and warn everyone. Then they sang a song, to show that people with depression can also have a rich inner world. It expressed an optimistic, upbeat spirit and encouraged everyone suffering from illness. Near the end of the gala, the audience suddenly got noisy. Someone had started handing out “countdown red envelopes”, but since I had left my phone on a seat nearby, I didn’t grab any.

After the gala, we walked out of the tent and once again faced the starry sky above Everest. A flash streaked across it, like a rocket. Soon we saw what it was: Mika, a creature from the game Seer. This Mika was strange. It had what looked like a solid shell, but any aircraft could fly through it without damaging it. Suddenly it clicked: this Mika must be made of the macro-atoms described in Liu Cixin’s Ball Lightning. They are huge, but their nuclei are tiny, so most things just pass through the hollow electron orbits and never hit the nucleus. Haha.

Suddenly, bright green bands of light appeared in the sky, shaking wildly. I thought excitedly, so this is the aurora? So beautiful…
