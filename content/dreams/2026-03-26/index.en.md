Today I dreamed I had become a time traveler again and gone back to my class in about third grade of primary school. All the kids were familiar faces. XN had traveled back with me. Oh right, at first K was there too. But after one class, K, who sat in the row in front of XN and me, suddenly vanished. His body and everything he owned were erased from this world in an instant. This wasn’t just a physical erasing. His existence had disappeared from the logic of this world: the kids no longer remembered that someone called K was in the class, and his effects on this world were gone too… Only XN and I knew that K, as a time traveler, had chosen to jump to another point in time. Maybe he found the class too boring. Strangely, after this class, my class 3-2 became class 3-4! Everyone thought they had always been in class 3-4, not 3-2. This was probably another sign that K’s effects on this world had been removed. Only XN and I remembered that we used to be in class 3-2. Then a kid held up a sheet of paper and asked our head teacher, Teacher C: “Why does the paper say class 3-2? Isn’t our class 3-4?” I was startled. Had the world only been partly changed?… This… didn’t seem to make sense. Teacher C looked confused too and said maybe it was a printing mistake. Later we found that the units of measure in the world had also changed a little… Only the ruler XN always carried still kept the old units… A 3.5 cm line measured with it really was 3.5 cm.
