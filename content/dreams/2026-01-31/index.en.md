Today I dreamed that during evening study hall, XF, our art class representative, suddenly announced that the art final project was due at midnight tonight. I instantly freaked out, because I hadn’t drawn a single line… So I rushed to start. First I sketched a parrot standing on a stick in pencil, then started coloring. I wanted its feathers to fade from light purple to dark purple. But I mixed and mixed and mixed for ages and couldn’t get the color. So I gave up and went with Plan B: a parrot with green feathers, with a little purple fading in at the wingtips. When I got to the tail, I had a sudden idea and gave it a bright red fade. Now I was happy with it. But the clock kept creeping toward midnight. I thought, teacher xxx is usually the type who doesn’t care much and has probably gone home long ago, so if I hand it in a little late, she probably won’t notice? Still, overall I kept painting faster. Next I needed a background. I picked a fairly simple world map: green for land, blue for sea, simple and clear. I hesitated a little, since a parrot in front of the Earth doesn’t quite make sense, but I pushed on and painted it anyway. Just before midnight, XF suddenly announced that we could also hand it in tomorrow morning. I let out a long breath.
