Today I dreamed I was in the woods on my primary school campus in early spring, with the snow not yet melted. I was chasing Gilles Brassard along a snow-covered path through the woods, but I soon lost him. At the edge of the woods I ran into LZX. We hadn’t seen each other in a long time. After catching up, I learned that she was now teaching French too. So we went to the youth center next door so she could give me a trial lesson and open my eyes. LZX taught very well. At one word, I suddenly thought it looked a lot like a German word I had seen before: a word related to planes that, with a prefix, could take on the meaning “halfway”. I checked my phone and found that what I had seen before was “furkairbus”, so “furk” must be the German prefix for “halfway”. LZX also told me that at two o’clock she had to go teach another class. I glanced at the clock: it was only 1:15, so there was probably time for one more lesson.
