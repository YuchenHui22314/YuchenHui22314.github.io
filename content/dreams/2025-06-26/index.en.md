In the second dream, I was at my middle school campus. Suddenly a building in view exploded! A dark red dust cloud began to spread slowly. CJJ’s voice came over the school speakers: “Students on floor 1.5 and above, please go into your classrooms and sit down.” She meant that the upper floors were far from the ground, so hiding in a classroom would do, while the lower floors were more dangerous and should evacuate. I looked at how high the dust cloud was and decided right away that she was talking nonsense. It was big enough to swallow the whole campus. So I got ready to escape by bike. I said to J next to me: “Think about it, it’s only about ten minutes home by bike, right?” He thought that made sense, so we made a run for it. On the way, I found that the bike under me had suddenly turned into a tiny unicycle, and every push on the pedal took a lot of effort. Even more annoying, the whole thing was made of cheap white plastic, and the handlebar kept coming loose from the frame, so I had to push it back in by hand. Luckily the dust cloud wasn’t spreading very fast, so we kept riding slowly like this. When we passed a crossroads, we looked left and saw lava (how did it turn into a volcanic eruption, haha) already very close. This road turned out to be the main road nearest to the exploding building, and the lava was rushing down it as if it were on a highway. J and I didn’t dare slow down and kept riding for our lives. Soon the area about 50 meters behind us was filled with thick white smoke glowing dark red. J dropped his bike and scanned a shared bike by the road. I thought, good idea! But then I thought, I’m already at a canteen not far from home, I can just run back. So I dropped my bike and ran like mad. After that, J and I went our separate ways, onto two different paths of fate. Then I woke up.

I fell asleep and the dream went on. I dreamed I got to the bottom of my building. Lots of people were waiting for the elevator. When we got in, it was overloaded, and a few people got out on their own. The elevator stopped on the second floor. When we stepped out, we (Y and I) seemed to have arrived in another world. In front of us hung two world maps. The continents were more crowded together than on a real world map, and their shapes were a bit twisted. When we leaned in to look at the details, I found that on my map the Chinese city names were unchanged, but the foreign city names had turned into characters I couldn’t read. Y’s map was different from mine: even the Chinese city names looked strange. Then an old man appeared beside us. He said the map reflected how a person thinks, a projection of the brain’s structure. We both looked at the maps, lost in thought. The old man took out a copybook so we could learn the script of this world. The copybook also used 米-shaped grids (squares with diagonal guide lines), but the strokes of this world’s characters could only run along the grid lines, so they looked very regular and were hard to tell apart. After a while the old man gave us a sticky red thing and said it was this world’s shuttlecock. In the hall next to us, lots of people really were hitting this red thing with rackets, so we took out our rackets and started playing too. Then I woke up again.

I fell asleep and it went on. I was waiting for the elevator again with some kids. They suggested we press the down button first, so we could catch the elevator before it reached the ground floor, and the people on the ground floor wouldn’t take all the room. We did that and all got in, but to our surprise, the elevator didn’t go down to the ground floor. It went straight up instead. Haha! Soon the elevator reached my floor, and I knocked on the door and was home. Dad was at home making dumplings for me. When I saw him, tears welled up in my eyes, and I went up and hugged him.
