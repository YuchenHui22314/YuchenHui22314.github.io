In the first dream, AD and I were competing in Olympic tennis on ice. First I warmed up in a small building next to the tennis court. The warm-up room was tiny, only about 2 × 2 m, and round. It had big floor-to-ceiling windows all around, so you could see the court in the distance. The floor was covered with the green mat used for badminton, and Coach S stood behind me. I started doing footwork drills and nearly ran into the windows several times. Coach S told me to be careful not to fall out.

After a while the competition began. Before the match, AD and I had agreed that he would knock everyone else out, reach the final, and then lose to me on purpose, so I would win Olympic gold. I watched AD play from the side. The ground was ice and very slippery, and AD used all kinds of badminton tricks, faking out the tennis guy on the other side until he questioned his whole life. On top of that, somehow a black curtain was hanging on the net, so the other side couldn’t see the moment AD hit the ball, which made things even worse for them. Soon it was me vs AD. Unlike what we had agreed, AD went all out from the start. I lost fast, and AD won the Olympic gold.

After that we needed to get home, so we started looking for a Communauto Flex car on the street.
