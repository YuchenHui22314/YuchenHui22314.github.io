Today, in the first dream, I dreamed I was woken up in my dream by the shrieking whistle of a kettle. Half asleep, I walked over and turned off the stove. Then it suddenly hit me: I didn’t boil any water before bed at all, so I must be dreaming right now. So I started straining my eyelids to open my eyes. But when I woke up the second time, the kettle was still there. I looked around. It was a nicely finished studio with an elegant wooden floor, in a classic peach-wood style a bit like a Chinese chess board, and a curved floor-to-ceiling window at the corner of the living room. Hmm, not bad at all. But I knew this was a dream, so I kept trying hard to open my eyes. Only after “waking up” several more times did I get back to the real world…
