The second dream. Z and I were sitting on the steps in front of a building. The building had a porch to shelter from the rain, and on top of the porch stood a beautiful big yellow bird. Z told me this bird had a nickname, “Yellow Hero” (the name of one of Lin Dan’s badminton shoes…). Everyone wanted to tame her, but no one had managed it. I looked up and admired the colorful tail feathers half hidden among Yellow Hero’s yellow plumage. Then I saw a lift next to the porch that went up to a platform level with the porch. So I took the lift up to the platform and leapt into the air, catching the bird on top of the porch in mid-air. I crashed hard onto the ground, but held on tight to the bird. Yellow Hero struggled in my arms a few times and then calmed down. I asked Z to take a photo of Yellow Hero and me, and then I would let her go. But while Z went to get the camera, Yellow Hero started struggling with all her might, clawing wildly. I ended up in a real mess, and in the end I released her back into the wild. Maybe some birds will always long for freedom.
