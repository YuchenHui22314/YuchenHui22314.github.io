In the second dream, my mom and I were on a train. The seats were in two rows, a bit like on a high-speed train. We sat in the right row. The left row seemed to be a VIP area, and ours was for regular passengers. The train kindly served dinner. After dinner, an attendant came to collect the plates. When the attendant got to our row, I saw it was L. L grinned at me and said, “Time to collect the dishes!” I laughed out loud too and handed everything to L: a small metal can we had been given earlier, plus 4 or 5 metal spoons. Then I needed the toilet and got up to look for one. First I went the way L had gone with the dishes, but at the connection between the cars there were too many people waiting. By then L had gone to the kitchen and was vaping and joking with other people. So I went the other way. The car in that direction had a light luxury style. It felt like the decoration in a government office building, with white walls and wooden door frames one after another. It looked quite nice. Just as I was about to walk through a door, a light above it came on to say the train was about to leave. Right after that, a door slowly slid out of the wall and blocked my way. Was the outside of the train already right there? It seemed this car was joined to a building outside, and the decoration really matched… I kept exploring this car and found a hall ahead, with a high upper floor inside! This… really didn’t look like something that could fit in a narrow space like a train. But I didn’t think much about it. I thought this must be where the train’s senior managers or some government officials worked, so there had to be a fancy toilet. So I carefully looked around for a toilet, a bit afraid that a train attendant would find me and send me away. I felt a little sneaky. When I got to the upper floor, I finally found the fancy toilet! Too bad lots of passengers were already waiting in line. There I also ran into ZZ and W, and the three of us started chatting. Soon one of us asked: “This place is so big, how can it be inside a train car?” The more we thought about it, the scarier it got. Had we never actually left, while the whole train went on without us? I quickly took out my phone to check where we were. It showed we had already reached the next station. Phew… But the whole thing felt eerie. When the train stopped again, one wall of the car slowly opened! A huge creature like a T. rex slowly walked into the hall. This wasn’t an office area, it was a cargo area! And the cargo might well be wild animals. I was so scared that I turned and ran. Then the dinosaur burst out laughing and said there was no need to run, I won’t hurt you… Me: ???
