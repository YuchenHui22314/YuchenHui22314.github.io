Today I dreamed I was studying at McGill. I slipped out of class to the door of the building, and S happened to walk by. I said, what a coincidence! He said, aren’t we both students at the tyler p**** business school? Then I noticed the black-and-red business school sign under the blue sky and white clouds. It really stood out. He went on to say that our track was related to medicine. On second thought, that was a bit more useful. After a while, class started. It took place outdoors, on a sheet of ice. The teacher was showing how to tell which way water flows from the ripples of water running over the ice. She said these ripples were called * lip marks, and that they usually run perpendicular to the direction of flow. Right after that, we were asked to use this result for an exercise in a blue textbook. I opened it: isn’t this Terence Tao’s Analysis? The exercise was actually to prove some theorems of set theory. That really stumped me. I also dreamed that the classmate next to me and I were writing on the ice.
