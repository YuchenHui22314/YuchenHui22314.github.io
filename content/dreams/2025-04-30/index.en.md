I woke up, fell asleep again and kept dreaming. I dreamed I had turned into a primary-school kid, like Conan. My family was on a road trip in Western Europe, with me and another kid. Dad was driving, and Mom sat in the passenger seat. The other kid and I sat in the back. When we reached a museum, the sky outside suddenly went dark. While everyone was in an uproar, it suddenly hit me: I think there’s a partial solar eclipse today! I rushed to the museum’s big floor-to-ceiling window. A black sun wrapped in a pale corona hung weakly in the night sky. Unlike in a total eclipse, a small patch at the lower right of the black disk gave off a silvery-white moonlight. The moonlit part and the shadowed part were split by a bright blue arc (the blue of a gas stove flame). Just as I was about to take out my phone, I saw the shadowed part slowly getting brighter and the moonlit part slowly getting darker. Too late. But then the partial eclipse entered its second stage. Both parts turned the color of dim yellow moonlight, and the dividing line slowly faded away. Now the sun looked like a slightly dimmer moon. I managed to capture the scene with my phone.
