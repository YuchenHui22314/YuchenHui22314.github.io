Today I dreamed I went back to my hometown. My family and a young woman were eating barbecue under the big awning of an outdoor barbecue place. Besides a huge spread of grilled food, we also ordered fried lotus root sandwiches. Later I dreamed that she, another young woman and I were doing a chemistry experiment. We slowly added an acid to a beaker of sodium nitrate. As the experiment went on, something strange happened: the solution slowly began to spin and formed a long, thin whirlpool! My brain raced. Maybe the liquid inside the beaker had started to convect, and the Coriolis force had turned its flow, a bit like how a typhoon forms. Then the young woman suggested that NaHNO3 was being formed, and that the large amount of heat released caused all this. Then she said one molecule of NaNO3 makes one molecule of NaHNO3. I said no, the formula of sodium hydrogen nitrate should be NaHNO2, so the equation still needed balancing.

Later I dreamed that Dad and I went swimming in a gorge in the hills outside my hometown. The river in the gorge was fairly calm, so the locals had fenced it off for playing in the water. Lots of people were splashing around. Dad and I went to the lower part of the gorge, planning to get in and swim upstream, which seemed to be the usual way the locals did it. Once we were in, Dad dove straight down and swam upstream along the bottom. I panicked a little, because I’m not good at swimming underwater. What now? Why is everyone swimming underwater, is this some convention too? That can’t be right. Can’t I just swim on the surface? With that, I swam upstream on the surface, doing breaststroke with my head up. I soon found that the current was very fast, and several times I went backward instead of forward. I was a bit scared, but with people all around me I figured I wouldn’t die, so I kept going and finally reached the end.

In my hometown I also ran into some of my middle school classmates, and QZX. I also met an actress who looked a lot like my mom. My family explained that the one who had kept me company when I was little was not actually my mom, but this actress. I was very confused and couldn’t see the point.

After that, I also dreamed that my grandma had graduated from my high school, and people were all talking about how I had graduated from there too, and so on.
