Once again, the classic plot of my soul traveling back to middle school. This time it was Teacher G’s English class. It seemed to be an open class, because my mom was sitting next to me observing. Like at any class she sat in on, she was taking careful notes. I soon found that my usual magenta fountain pen was missing; it turned out she was using it. So I had to take notes with a light blue fountain pen I wasn’t used to. After a few words I couldn’t stand it and swapped pens with her. Teacher G wrote the words on the blackboard one by one and explained each one. Suddenly the bell rang, and Teacher G stopped right there. I fell into thought: if I were the teacher, would I finish the rest or leave it for next time? We were almost done with the text… For the teacher, finishing is convenient: no need to contextualize it again next time. But for students, once the bell rings, their minds are gone. So I quietly decided that if I ever become a teacher, I’ll end class on time. Besides, I see online that many teachers are even happier than the students when the bell rings, so there’s no need to run over.

During the break I turned around to play Seer with the classmate behind me. LXY and LHC watched. I started a 6v6 Peak Holy War match, but it opened as 3v3. Fine, fine, 3v3 it is. I led with the Golden Pegasus of Holy Light against something on the other side (I forget what). I used Holy Legacy, and the other player mindlessly assumed I was about to buff, so they switched in Xiang Yu to strip my buffs and lock me down with Burnout, and their Xiang Yu went down. LXY said, didn’t think you were this good! Then, when I wanted to use Wonderland Memory, I found that this account’s Pegasus carried four attack moves and no status moves at all, which threw me. So I just used Extreme Aurora and got picked off by a creature they switched in. Strangely, my side then showed two of my creatures dead, when only one had died… But the real problem was that Teacher G had already confiscated an iPad at the back of the classroom, because those students were gaming together too. So we got nervous and scattered.

The next class was math, taught by Teacher H. Halfway through, my mom fell asleep. Teacher H angrily asked who was sitting next to me and why they were sleeping. I hurried to explain that it was my mom. Teacher H said, I don’t care, I just don’t like anyone sleeping in my class, don’t let me see it again, and so on.

Later it seemed to be Teacher G’s class again. I sat in the left column of the third group, with my mom on my right. Behind me to the left, around the fourth row of the fourth group, sat R and her boyfriend. Suddenly I heard R groan in pain. I turned and saw her clutching her stomach, head down, brow furrowed, in pain. Teacher G came over from the front, talked with them briefly, said coldly, “Then go to the hospital quickly,” and went back to teaching. I thought, how can Teacher G be so cold? R looks really unwell… it must… be serious. I watched her boyfriend help her out of the classroom with difficulty, and suddenly noticed he was shorter than her. I also saw both of their long, strong athlete’s legs and felt a bit envious. But then a flash went through my mind: I suddenly remembered that today was the last day my past self ever saw R in our class. After that she seemed to vanish. Rumor said she was pregnant, but with pain like this, could it be an ectopic pregnancy or something? Then things probably didn’t end well for R. Teacher G didn’t take it seriously, and it really seemed to have hurt R badly. But unlike in my memory, this time R didn’t just leave; after a while she came back to the classroom with her boyfriend. I thought, here’s my chance, this time I’ll save her and change history. So I went up and asked how she was feeling. She pointed to several spots on her chest, shoulders and neck, and said things I didn’t quite understand. I froze for a moment: is it because they’re Thai and their Chinese isn’t good yet? But from the spots she pointed to, I suddenly thought of a symptom called the “three depressions sign” (I only remembered the name after waking up; in the dream I just made the connection). I didn’t remember exactly what it meant, but I was sure things were already serious. So I put on a stern face and said, “We have to go to the hospital.” But now R got coy and didn’t want to go. She said she had just asked the girl behind her, whose parents are doctors, and was told it was nothing. I got anxious and shouted: you can’t wait with this, this could be… (I looked at her boyfriend and couldn’t say “ectopic pregnancy”) …anyway, either you come to the hospital with me, or you go yourself! Seeing how fierce I looked, R got angry too and actually stormed out of the classroom. Her boyfriend was fairly responsible and chased after her with me.

On the way, I realized this was exactly the road to Drum Tower Hospital, and I thought she had chosen the “go yourself” option, which comforted me a little. But the road to the hospital wasn’t quite like in real life: you had to cross a very high footbridge. How absurd was this footbridge? First, it was high, about seven or eight stories. Second, it was hard to get onto: first you climb a platform about 1.5 m high, then a very long spiral staircase with tall steps, then a rope up to the bridge deck. The deck also had gaps everywhere; you had to swing across on a bar from one side to grab the bar on the other side, or do a standing long jump over the abyss. A nightmare for anyone afraid of heights. Her boyfriend climbed onto the platform ahead of me, and I steeled myself and vaulted up too. Then came the spiral staircase, and her boyfriend suddenly slowed down. I cursed him silently: hurry up, we can’t even see her anymore… R had run fast and was long gone; she was probably already swinging on the bars, which was really dangerous in her condition. I grew more and more anxious and thought, what a coward this boyfriend is! At last we reached the rope, and now he was quick, up in a flash. But just as I was about to climb, I saw that the rope (a hemp rope) was fraying and looked about to snap. So I pulled the rope down a bit, so that I could hold the frayed spot in my hand and keep it from taking the load. Suddenly I heard ZZH behind me shout, how wide is the standing long jump? Right after, LLD’s voice came out of nowhere: “As wide as each person is tall.” Maybe LLD was somewhere ahead, but I couldn’t tell which direction the voice came from. ZZH didn’t seem to believe it and asked again, and LLD answered again. Then I woke up.

I hope she’s okay…

After going to the bathroom, I lay back in bed and kept going over the dream as I drifted off. I fell asleep. In the dream, JS and I were going to Drum Tower Hospital together. I said, we absolutely can’t take the footbridge, let’s take the underpass. In the underpass I told JS R’s story. When we got to the hospital, we sat in a compartment that looked a lot like a sleeper car, with some old men in the upper and lower bunks, and I kept telling the story vividly. JS and another old man sat on the lower bunk across from me, listening closely. I noticed an electronic screen on the wall with lines of black text, which looked like personal information, maybe a queue display… Then, just as I got to the standing long jump part, my alarm went off.
