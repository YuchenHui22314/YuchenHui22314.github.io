Today I dreamed I drove a speedboat into a big lake. It was already late autumn, and now and then there was floating ice on the lake. I was holding a Minecraft-style parchment map, and a bird’s-eye view of the lake appeared on it. Like Lake Balkhash, the whole lake was split into a saltwater part and a freshwater part. The saltwater part was called the Tethys Ocean (lol). I forget what the freshwater part was called. In the middle of the saltwater part floated a permanent ice sheet, marked in white. The freshwater part had none. I was going from the freshwater part into the saltwater part. I saw more and more ice breaking off the permanent ice sheet as a storm pulled at it, like a big flatbread being torn apart. The floes were about 2 to 3 meters thick. The ice wasn’t pure white. It was mixed with lots of black sand and gravel from who knows where, and it looked a lot like a glacier on land. A strong current carried them in a huge procession from the saltwater part to the freshwater part. Later I landed on the shore at the far north end of the saltwater part and went ashore. Then I thought, wait, once the lake freezes in winter, the ice will crush my boat. That’s why mine was the only boat on the lake. But I didn’t know how to get the boat out of the water. Maybe a long time passed. When I opened the map in my hand again, white covered the whole lake. That meant the lake was completely frozen! I rushed to the shore, but it was still open water. Looking closer, though, I noticed a faint, almost clear layer of ice on the surface, like slush soaking in the water. The lake was frighteningly still, and a thin white mist rose from nowhere, as if to announce that winter had come… (I think there was also a part in the middle where I walked on melting ice.)
