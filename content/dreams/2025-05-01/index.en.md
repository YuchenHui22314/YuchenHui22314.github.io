Today I dreamed I was back in my middle school class. The setting seemed to be New York, and right outside the window was that river in New York. It was the middle of winter, and chunks of ice drifted by, bobbing in the river, and kept catching my eye from inside the classroom. Teacher G was holding a class meeting about keeping the classroom clean. The meeting was quite democratic: everyone could speak freely and give suggestions. Someone stood up and complained that I didn’t mop the floor carefully, or something like that. It made me laugh in disbelief, so I raised my hand too. First I covered myself: thank you, classmate xxx, I’ll be sure to pay attention from now on. But it’s strange. I remember that at the last class meeting a classmate praised me for mopping very carefully, and now I’m being told I’m careless. I don’t even know anymore whether I’m careful or not. Everyone burst out laughing, and Teacher G smoothed things over.

A little later the next class was about to start: physics with Teacher B. Before physics, our math homework was handed back. While handing it out, the group leader insisted on looking at everyone’s grade. I got a C, with every calculation question wrong. But I wasn’t worried. It’s like this every time there’s calculation homework anyway.

A while later, during class, the handsome American guy behind me told me the river outside had frozen over. I quickly turned around and saw the Hudson River filled with huge icebergs. Even better, lots of penguins were hopping along between the icebergs. It seemed the New York authorities had opened a polar zoo on the river while the ice was solid. I wanted to go right away, but class wasn’t over yet. I wondered why the river had suddenly frozen. A possibility flashed through my mind: maybe the tide had carried sea ice into the estuary? But when I looked at the current, I saw the river was still flowing toward the sea. After a while, the American guy and I started chatting. I asked where he was from. He said Kentucky, where it’s very warm and never snows all year. He was about to go home for the holidays.
