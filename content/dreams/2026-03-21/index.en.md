Today I dreamed that we were walking around a campus at night. Suddenly all the building lights and street lamps around us went dark, and a real pitch-black night arrived. Then an aurora appeared in the sky! Green mixed with purple, it looked like a thin veil glowing softly in the sky, swaying slowly in a light breeze. Now and then the purple faded away, which made us treasure it even more. Without a word, we all took out our phones to record the beautiful sight. Soon the roar of engines echoed through the sky. Following the sound, I saw a large, shining plane about to land. Someone next to me said it was Air Force One, the US president’s plane, coming in to land. So maybe that was why the lights around us had suddenly gone out: for security. I thought, that doesn’t seem right? With everything dark, won’t the plane look even brighter… What an obvious target.

I dreamed I was in Teacher L’s computer class, which suddenly turned into a music class. In class we were listening to Chang Chen-yue’s song “First Taste of Love”. Without noticing, I started singing along, and the whole class turned to look at me. But I seemed to have forgotten some of the lyrics, so I stumbled my way to the end of the song. Maybe I just needed to let it out, haha.

Later I dreamed I was having dinner with some people at a fancy restaurant. Before dinner, I played a special version of Chinese chess off to the side with someone (I can’t remember who). The board was shaped like a U, with the palaces at the two ends of the U, and it seemed only as wide as a palace, just two or three rows. The cannons seemed able to cross the empty space in the middle of the U to reach the other end, but the horses and chariots couldn’t.
