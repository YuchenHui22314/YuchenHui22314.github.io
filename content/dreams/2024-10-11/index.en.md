The first dream: our class was on a spring outing. One of the activities was a tag game. At the start there was one catcher, and everyone else was being chased. As the game went on, anyone who got caught became a catcher too. You can guess that the game ends when everyone has been caught and turned into the catcher’s helpers. Halfway through, the teacher suddenly called a break. When the game started again, LTC walked up to me with his trademark smile. I thought, as far as I remember, this guy hadn’t been caught in the first round… At that moment I was holding an enchanted iron sword; one swing at him would deal 10 damage. QK was sticking with me and also held a weapon that dealt 10 damage. Everyone, catcher or not, had only 20 HP, so the two of us together could take down any catcher. But LTC just narrowed his eyes and chatted with us, and we thought he was on our side. Then suddenly he said, you’re caught. Only then did we realize we had been tricked. But seeing nobody else around, he pulled us aside and said, I’ll give you another chance. Your best strategy is actually to trust nothing anyone says and hit anyone you meet with two swings straight away. That way, whatever side they are on, they will be taken out. It doesn’t matter if they aren’t a catcher, because if you take out everyone, the game ends with you winning. It’s a sure-win strategy! QK and I thought that made sense. I forget how the rest of the game went, haha.
